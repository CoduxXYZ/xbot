Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

By using Lovebyte you consent to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the bot. Lovebyte follows the Discord Developer Terms of Service and Developer Policy.

In short

  • Lovebyte only collects what it needs to run its features.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • Discord data is never used to train AI models.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data.

2

Where usernames appear

Leaderboards, balances, levels, boosts, confession logs, snipe and other lists show Discord usernames or mentions to other members of the Lovesick server. See xhelp [command] for what each command displays.

3

How we use data

We use the data only to:

  • run Lovebyte’s features
  • keep the economy and levels fair
  • support moderation and tickets
  • fix bugs and improve the bot

We do not use message content or other Discord data to train machine learning or AI models, we do not build profiles of users for advertising or marketing, and we do not contact you outside Discord.

4

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or license your data. We never share Discord data with data brokers, advertising networks or any monetisation service.

Data is visible to others only through the features listed above (for example, a leaderboard). Lovesick moderators can view tickets and confession logs. We may disclose data if the law requires it or to protect people from harm.

Data is stored with our hosting and database providers, who process it solely on our behalf to run the bot.

5

How long we keep it

We keep data only as long as it is needed to run Lovebyte’s features. We delete it when it is no longer needed or when you ask us to.

  • Economy, level, boost, SquishMallows and profile data: kept while you are a member of the Lovesick server, unless you ask us to delete it.
  • Deleted messages used by snipe: kept for a short time only, then discarded.
  • Tickets, confessions and suggestions: kept as long as Lovesick staff need them for moderation or support, then removed on request.
  • If Lovebyte is permanently shut down, all stored data will be deleted within a reasonable period (normally within 60 days).
6

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you by opening a ticket in the Lovesick server. Include your Discord user ID so we can locate your data.

Some records (for example past moderation actions) may be retained if needed to keep the community safe.

7

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect data, but no system is perfectly secure. Do not share sensitive personal information through Lovebyte. If we become aware of unauthorised access to Discord data, we will notify Discord and affected users as required by the Discord Developer Terms.

8

Children

Lovebyte is not intended for anyone under Discord’s minimum age. If you believe a child under that age has used Lovebyte, contact us via ticket and we will delete their data.

9

Discord and this policy

Discord handles your data under its own Privacy Policy. This policy only covers the data Lovebyte collects through the Discord API.

10

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of Lovebyte after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Questions or data requests

Join the Lovesick server and open a ticket, or message a member of staff. Include your Discord user ID for data requests.
