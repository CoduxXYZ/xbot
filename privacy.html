Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

xBot only collects what it needs to run its features. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. xBot follows the Discord Developer Terms of Service and Developer Policy.

xBot does not collect passwords, payment details, email addresses, phone numbers or direct messages, and it does not read private conversations.

2. Where usernames appear

Leaderboards, balances, levels, boosts, confessions logs, snipe and other lists show Discord usernames or mentions to other members of the Lovesick server. See xhelp [command] for what each command displays.

3. How we use data

We use data only to run xBot’s features, keep the economy and levels fair, support moderation and tickets, and fix bugs. We do not use message content or other Discord data to train machine learning or AI models, we do not build profiles of users, and we do not contact you outside Discord.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or license your data, and we never share Discord data with data brokers, advertising networks or any monetisation service. Data is visible to others only through the features above (for example, a leaderboard). Lovesick moderators can view tickets and confession logs. We may disclose data if the law requires it or to protect people from harm. Data is stored with our hosting and database providers, who process it on our behalf.

5. How long we keep it

We keep data only as long as it is needed to run xBot’s features. We delete it when it is no longer needed or when you ask us to.

  • Economy, level, boost, SquishMallows and profile data: kept while you are a member of the Lovesick server, unless you ask us to delete it.
  • Deleted messages used by snipe: kept for a short time only, then discarded.
  • Tickets, confessions and suggestions: kept as long as Lovesick staff need them, then removed on request.
  • If xBot is shut down, its stored data is deleted within a reasonable period.

6. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you by opening a ticket in the Lovesick server. Include your Discord user ID so we can find it. Some data (such as the record of a past moderation action) may be kept if it is needed to keep the community safe.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect data, but no system is perfectly secure. Do not share sensitive personal information through xBot. If we become aware of unauthorised access to Discord data, we will notify Discord and affected users as the Discord Developer Terms require.

8. Children

xBot is not meant for anyone under Discord’s minimum age. If you believe a child under that age has used xBot, contact us and we will delete their data.

9. Discord and this policy

Discord handles your data under its own Privacy Policy. This policy covers only the data xBot collects through the Discord API.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Questions or data requests

Join the Lovesick server and open a ticket, or message a member of staff. Include your Discord user ID for data requests.
