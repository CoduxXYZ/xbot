Lovebyte website (Terms of Service + Privacy Policy)

Files
  index.html    Home page
  404.html      Page-not-found page (GitHub Pages uses it automatically)
  terms.html    Terms of Service
  privacy.html  Privacy Policy
  css/style.css Styles
  js/legal.js   Highlights the current section on the Terms/Privacy pages
  assets/       Logo, banner and divider images

Hosting: upload the whole folder to any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages).
Discord Developer Portal > your app > General Information: set the
  Terms of Service URL   to  https://YOUR-DOMAIN/terms.html
  Privacy Policy URL     to  https://YOUR-DOMAIN/privacy.html
